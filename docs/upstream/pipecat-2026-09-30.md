mygpt uses Pipecat only as an optional realtime transport/frame boundary. Finalized transcription is passed into CompanionChatRuntime, while persona, memory, Book context and supervision policy remain owned by mygpt.

The integration deliberately avoids the deprecated PipelineTask/PipelineRunner surface and does not make Pipecat authoritative for companion state.
